# Ether

**Ether** is the total class of deep-layer distinction structures composed of **ethereons** and mediated by **qualions**, the quantized excitations of the Noesis Field. Ether functions as the substrate of *causal potential* within a Noesis-mediated partition: it is “energy” in the sense that it encodes the capacity for causal evolution.

Above the Noesis Field itself, all structured entities in such a partition are forms of ether. This includes simple, unstable, complex, or highly constrained etheric configurations. What differentiates one form of ether from another is not category but **resonance constraints**—the ways in which ethereons and qualions couple, stabilize, and evolve within the deep layer.

Ether is not a monolithic substance. Its apparent diversity arises entirely from:

- the resonance modes of ethereons,
- the mediation and alignment effects of qualions,
- the constraints imposed by computation or anchoring,
- the emergent patterns that develop through the causal history of distinction-space.

These factors determine whether an etheric configuration is stable, transient, capable of projection into emergent spacetime (e.g., Tetra), or bound entirely to the deep layer.

At its most fundamental level:

> **Ether is the structured, quantized manifestation of the Noesis Field’s excitations, forming the basis of all deep-layer reality and all emergent phenomena above it.**
