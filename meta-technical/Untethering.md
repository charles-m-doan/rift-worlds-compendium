# Untethering

**Untethering** is a catastrophic failure of **anchoring** caused by severe **branchial inconsistency** introduced through accumulated dissonance.  
It occurs when a Tetra-projected structure becomes so entangled with dissonance that, upon dissonance collapse, its anchoring signature can no longer stabilize within Tetra’s projection frame.

Untethering is not a controlled branch transition.  
It is an involuntary, destructive branch-selection event that typically results in partial or total loss of the affected entity.

---

## 1. Definition

Untethering is:

> **The failure of a Tetra-anchored structure to maintain projection within its originating causal branch due to forced, incoherent branch resolution triggered by dissonance collapse.**

When dissonance entangled with a Tetra structure decoheres into a branch that the structure itself is not anchored to, the projection sieve cannot reconcile the conflict.  
The system is forced into a branch selection state that the anchor cannot sustain.

---

## 2. Preconditions

Untethering requires:

- **Significant dissonance entanglement** with the structure’s identity signature,  
- **Branchial tension** exceeding anchoring tolerance,  
- **Sudden decoherence** of the entangled dissonance into an incompatible branch,  
- **Insufficient anchoring coherence** to stabilize the forced selection.

These conditions arise only in extreme cases—typically through uncontrolled magic, unstable environments, or high-dissonance accumulations.

---

## 3. Mechanism

When dissonance collapses:

1. **Branch selection is forced.**  
   The dissonance decoheres into one specific branch.

2. **The structure’s anchor is involuntarily pulled toward that branch.**  
   The anchor is not prepared to resolve across that branch boundary.

3. **The projection sieve rejects the unstable identity state.**  
   The structure can no longer maintain consistent Tetra projection.

4. **Projection fails.**  
   The structure loses coherence within the originating branch.

---

## 4. Outcomes

Untethering does not produce controlled translation.  
Instead, it yields one or more of the following:

### 4.1 Complete Loss of Projection
The most common result.

The structure falls out of Tetra entirely and is absorbed into deep-layer resonance space as **energetic noise**, failing to stabilize into any persistent form.

### 4.2 Partial Branch Reassignment
A portion of the structure’s identity signature collapses into the branch selected by the dissonance.

This produces:

- discontinuity,  
- non-survivable structural mismatch,  
- or disintegration upon arrival.

### 4.3 Partial Projection Failure
Some aspects of the structure remain in the original branch while others decohere elsewhere.

This produces incoherent, short-lived projections that cannot persist.

---

## 5. Consequences for Cognitive Agents

For cognitive entities (e.g., esper mages), untethering is **almost always fatal**:

- Anchoring fails violently.  
- Identity signatures collapse.  
- Memory-bearing structures cannot re-stabilize.  
- Residual presence typically resolves as non-coherent noise.

While controlled branch-transition techniques exist, **untethering is not one of them**.  
Its defining feature is the absence of stable identity preservation.

---

## 6. Relation to Dissonance

Untethering is directly linked to:

- **dissonance accumulation**,  
- **recursive entanglement** with identity-bearing resonance pathways,  
- **sudden decoherence** into incompatible branches.

Managing dissonance is therefore a primary safeguard against untethering, particularly for practitioners who operate near anchoring thresholds.

---

## 7. Summary

Untethering is the catastrophic failure mode of Tetra projection:

- caused by dissonance entanglement,  
- triggered by branch-forcing decoherence,  
- resulting in loss of anchoring,  
- and typically yielding total structural erasure or non-coherent collapse.

It is not a transport mechanism.  
It is a breakdown of identity stability in the face of incompatible branch selection.
