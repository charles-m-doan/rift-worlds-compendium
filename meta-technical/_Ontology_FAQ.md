# Riftworlds Ontology FAQ

## FAQ Entry #1 — Magic, Tetra, and Observer-Relative Visibility

### Q:
In the ontological ordering, does **Tetra** emerge before or after **magic**, given that magic appears only through projection into Tetra?

### A:
Magic, as a **deep-layer process**, emerges *before* Tetra.  
Magic, as a **visible phenomenon to a Tetra-bound observer**, emerges *after* Tetra.

Deep-layer magical processes — resonance shaping, metachemical assembly, and Noesis-driven computation — do not depend on Tetra’s existence. They operate whenever a Noesis Field and etheric substrate are present.

However, **Tetra-bound observers** can perceive magical effects only after a Tetra sieve stabilizes to project those deep-layer patterns into 4D spacetime.

Other observers — pre-Tetra observers, or observers embedded in non-Tetra emergent geometries within the same Noesis partition — may perceive deep-layer magical behavior directly without any need for Tetra projection. Visibility is always **layer-relative** to the observer’s sieve.

**Hybrid observers** (e.g., humans or other entities who develop hyperdimensional extensions or deep-layer resonance pathways) may perceive *both* pre-Tetra magic and Tetra-projected magic simultaneously.  
This expanded perceptual bandwidth **does not change the causal ordering** — it only increases the observer’s access to deep-layer processes that previously lay beneath their visibility threshold.

Thus:

- **Magic (deep layer)** precedes Tetra.  
- **Magic (Tetra-visible)** follows Tetra.  
- **Hybrid observers** simply experience additional layers of the same causal chain.
