**Rift**
*A rift is a localized region of spacetime where the flux level rises above a critical threshold, causing 4-dimensional spacetime to uplift smoothly into a higher-dimensional throat. Rifts form long-lived, traversible connections between distant regions of the universe and are anchored to massive bodies or hyperdimensional structures, often remaining stable for billions to trillions of years.*

Rifts arise from the underlying **fractal causal substrate** of spacetime. In this substrate, effective dimensionality is not fixed but emerges from local fractal density. **Flux level** is the scalar that measures this density: low flux corresponds to an effective dimensionality close to 4, while higher flux allows additional spatial degrees of freedom to become expressible. When flux crosses the **phase threshold**, a throat forms—a region where higher-dimensional behavior becomes dominant while still presenting a stable, navigable 4D submanifold to macroscopic observers.

Each rift generates an extended **flux halo** in the surrounding environment. Flux decreases exponentially with distance from the throat yet never reaches zero, ensuring that any world containing a rift has a **non-negligible global flux floor**. Worlds with multiple rifts exhibit higher average ambient flux due to overlapping halos, but **diminishing returns** prevent ambient flux from ever approaching the much higher flux levels found inside a throat. Effective dimensionality therefore varies smoothly from near-4D far from the rift, to elevated dimensionality near the throat, to fully higher-dimensional behavior within the throat interior.

Rift interiors function as **exotic biomes**: environments that partly echo the physical and chemical conditions of the worlds they connect, while also exhibiting unique properties driven by their elevated fractal density and strong coupling to the deeper field. Over astronomical timescales, these interiors develop independent geographies, energy cycles, and evolutionary pathways shaped by the high-flux regime.

On **ultra-low-flux worlds**, the deeper field remains dormant under ordinary conditions; however, temporary or localized flux surges can briefly raise coupling above the interaction threshold, enabling limited Noesis Field phenomena even in regions without permanent rifts.

Rifts collectively form **interconnected networks** of stable, higher-dimensional passages, allowing traversal between worlds that would otherwise be causally isolated in 4D spacetime.
