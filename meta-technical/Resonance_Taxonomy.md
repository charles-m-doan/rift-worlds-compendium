# Resonance Taxonomy

Resonances are **not fundamental features of reality**. They are **emergent, coarse-grained observables**, comparable to temperature or gravity, used by human observers to reason about complex causal structures.

Nothing described here constitutes ontological bedrock. Any use of terms such as *fundamental* reflects **in-setting human interpretation**, not an absolute claim about reality.

---

## 1. Ontological framework (non-negotiable assumptions)

- There are **no fundamental particles, fields, forces, or spacetime**.
- “Spacetime” is shorthand for a coarse-grained stabilization of a deeper causal network.
- That causal network is itself emergent, arising from recursive patterns of distinction and constraint.
- There is no global or god’s-eye description of reality—only local, context-dependent regularities.
- Explanation never bottoms out; all abstraction layers are provisional.

Resonances describe **tendencies of causal transformation**, not entities or building blocks.

---

## 2. Resonances: general properties

- Resonances are **measurement axes**, not partitions of reality.
- All systems manifest **all resonances** at non-zero magnitude under coarse observation.
- Differences arise from **relative composition**, not exclusivity.
- Full-resolution resonance structure is computationally irreducible; observers necessarily work with compressed descriptions.

There are **12 resonances** relevant to human-observable spacetimes:
- 1 Null Resonance  
- 4 Primordial Resonances  
- 7 Natural Resonances  

These categories persist because they remain *useful*, not because they are true.

---

## 3. Null resonance

### Lachis — null

- Absence of stable causal pattern
- Cancellation or suppression of resonant structure

Lachis represents the **failure of pattern stabilization**, not an active tendency. Lachis can be thought of as the convergent tendency of Distinction-Space partioning. As the restrictiveness of a partition approaches zero—i.e. as relevance constraints are relaxed—the capacity for stable causal pattern collapses. In this limit, the partition’s resonance approaches perfect Lachis, representing the absence of any stabilized informational tendency.

---

## 4. Primordial resonances  
*(informational-tendency layer)*

Primordial resonances describe tendencies in **informational patterning**.  
“Information” here is not fundamental; it emerges from recursive distinctions that create context and contrast. Information is always **local**.

### Aidis — preservation
- Structural buffering
- Resistance to loss of meaning
- Analogous to neutral or non-functional mutations

### Eris — alteration
- Meaning-altering change
- Structural variation
- Analogous to functional mutations

### Clothis — integration
- Increasing information density
- Integration and tension-balance between Aidis and Eris
- Commonly associated with life and complex organization

### Atris — disintegration
- Decreasing information density
- Structural collapse or simplification
- Extremes of Aidis or Eris
- Commonly associated with death

Moral interpretations (good/evil, law/chaos) are **human projections**, not intrinsic properties.  
Local Atris may serve global Clothis, and vice versa, depending on scale and context.

---

## 5. Natural resonances  
*(spacetime-interface layer)*

Natural resonances are **coarse-grained proxies** for how strongly systems couple to recurring patterns of causal transformation in spacetime-like regimes.

They do not correspond to discrete components of reality and do not imply ontological primacy.

### 5.1 Pancosmic resonances  
*(relevant to arbitrary spacetimes)*

- **Chronos** — time  
  Temporal ordering, duration, causal sequencing.

- **Topos** — space  
  Spatial relations, extension, position, geometry.

- **Quantos** — quantity  
  Amount, multiplicity, aggregation, discrete vs continuous structure.

These resonances remain meaningful across a wide range of possible cosmological systems.

---

### 5.2 Idiocosmic resonances  
*(specific to human-compatible spacetime, “Terra”)*

- **Gravitos** — mass  
  Inertia, aggregation, spacetime curvature.  
  Nearly pancosmic, but with system-dependent behavior.

- **Elektros** — electricity  
  Electrical phenomena and electromagnetic coupling.

- **Thermos** — temperature  
  Thermal motion and energy distribution.

- **Lumos** — light  
  Radiative interactions and luminous intensity.  
  Likely emergent from composite resonance patterns rather than primitive.

Other cosmological systems may possess additional idiocosmic resonances that are inaccessible or meaningless to humans, analogous to inaccessible internal states of black holes.

---

## 6. Resonance signatures

All systems possess **resonance signatures** encoding their unique causal history within the space of possibilities.

At full resolution these signatures are irreducible.  
Macroscopic observers necessarily measure them in compressed form, which collapses naturally to the 12-resonance framework.

---

## 7. Final clarification

Resonances are **not features of reality itself**, but stable, historically contingent abstraction axes used by human civilizations to reason about observable regularities arising from deeper, non-fundamental processes.

They endure because they remain predictive—not because they are true.
