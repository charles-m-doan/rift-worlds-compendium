# Glyphons and Onticons

In Rift Worlds there are two fundamentally different modes of information storage and use. Glyphons preserve information by representation—symbols, marks, encodings, or patterns impressed onto a substrate—while onticons preserve information by participation, sustaining structured metaphysical linkages that are not symbolic but constitutive. The distinction is not one of medium or complexity, but of ontology: glyphic data describes, records, or models states of affairs, whereas ontic data is an active extension of those states. Understanding this divide clarifies why some artifacts can be read, copied, and transmitted, while others can only function through maintained entanglement and direct metaphysical connection.

## Glyphons
An object that stores *glyphic* (representational, symbolic) information by inscription, encoding, or modulation of a substrate. This is essentially information storage as we know it.

### Examples of glyphons
- clay tablet with cuneiform impressions  
- sheet of paper covered in ink symbols  
- carved wooden tally-stick  
- wax tablet with stylus marks  
- magnetic tape reel holding encoded audio  
- USB flash drive storing digital files  
- punched card with patterned holes  
- crystal slate etched with runic data  
- ceramic disc fired with micro-inscriptions  
- polished metal plate engraved with diagrams  
- photochemical film strip storing images  
- polymer sheet with encoded optical pits (like a CD)  
- coded textile with woven signal patterns  

## Onticons
An object that stores *ontic* (entangled, ground-of-being-level) information by sustaining or extending chains of intrinsic linkage at a metaphysical level. The *mapping formats* of onticons vary as widely as physical glyphons do. A focus, anchor stone, or energist differs in function and form, but not in the underlying ontic principle. The physical object is an anchoring interface, and different geometries are better suited to different entanglement meshes.

### Examples of onticons
- anchor stone (for spatial folding / hyper pathways)  
- resonance stone (for resonance profiles and resonance signatures)  
- focus (bound to hyper systems such as spell modules and ether pools)  
- phylactery (a special kind of focus specifically for esper cores)  
- energist (bound to ether pools of various compositions)  

## Ontic-to-glyphic mapping

### Ontic data → glyphic data (lossy mapping)
A glyphon can capture *some* aspects of an ontic structure: patterns, measurements, resonance traces, positional readings, symbolic encodings of observed behaviors. These are shadows or projections of the underlying structure.

### Glyphic data ← never a full representation
A glyphon cannot encode the full entangled state because the ontic structure is not representational; its informational content *is* the entanglement itself. Symbolic capture inevitably collapses or omits the intrinsic linkages that constitute the ontic identity.

### Map vs Territory
Glyphic = a map  
Ontic = an active piece of the terrain

### Resulting implications
- Onticons contain information that cannot be fully “read out.”  
- Glyphic renderings always omit causal depth, cross-linkage, and underlying continuity.  
- A complete ontic state cannot be reconstructed from glyphic records.  
- Scholars can map, approximate, classify, or model ontic systems, but cannot reproduce them without extending their actual entanglement chains.  
- Ontic data is the only kind of data capable of establishing or modifying spacetime reference-frame relationships. It does not represent these relationships; it directly participates in them as entangled structure.
- Glyphic data can describe reference-frame behavior but cannot anchor, bind, or stabilize anything within spacetime. It lacks ontic continuity.
- The term **onticon** does not refer to all objects that possess ontic structure (which is everything). It refers specifically to objects intentionally shaped or configured so that their ontic structure is usable as a stable metaphysical linkage point.
- An onticon is therefore a specialized artifact whose entanglement pattern can serve as the foundation for magic, hyper-system operations, resonance work, spatial folding, or other frame-dependent phenomena.
- Ordinary objects have ontic structure, but only onticons have ontic structure that is accessible, intensified, or engineered for metaphysical interaction.
