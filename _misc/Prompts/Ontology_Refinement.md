# RIFT WORLDS — ONTOLOGY REFINEMENT TASK DESCRIPTION

## Purpose
The goal is to build a unified, substrate-first worldbuilding ontology for the Rift Worlds setting.  
The ontology must be clean, coherent, minimal, and generative, supporting physics, magic, cosmology, metaphysics, biology, and narrative systems without contradictions or arbitrary exceptions.

This is an iterative subtractive design process: the system should evolve toward a state where nothing can be removed without breaking coherence.

## Core Design Objectives

### 1. Refine and Consolidate Existing Documents
The knowledge base contains multiple interrelated documents (e.g., Distinction-Space, Noesis Field, Ether, Ethereons, Essences, Metachemistry, Hyperchemistry, Magic, Rifts, Tetraspace, etc.).  
These documents may overlap, re-explain concepts, or contain ambiguous or autogenerated sections.

Target operations:

- identify redundancies  
- remove contradictions  
- clarify ambiguous relationships  
- unify terminology  
- compress repeated explanations  
- preserve only minimal, essential structure  

### 2. Maintain a Clear Hierarchical Ontological Ordering
The ontology must respect this causal ordering:

Distinction → Structured Computation → Noesis Field → Etheric Structures → Essences → Magic / Metachemistry / Hyperchemistry → Tetra (Tetraspace / Terra coupling) → Rifts / Higher Phenomena

No document, concept, or revision may contradict this ordering.

### 3. Preserve Substrate Independence and Layered Emergence
Principles:

- Distinction-space is the only primitive.  
- All deeper layers are emergent.  
- No emergent layer is final.  
- Structures (e.g., Noesis, Ether, Tetra) have identity independent of the substrate that realizes them.  
- Magical and physical behavior must always arise from stabilized deeper structure, not from arbitrary, ad hoc rules.

### 4. Avoid Premature Merging or Rewriting
Refinement should be incremental.

- Large reorganizations or merged documents should occur only after key ambiguities are clarified.  
- An authoritative FAQ / arbitration layer should exist and be consulted before major structural rewrites.

### 5. Build an Authoritative FAQ / Clarification Layer
As ambiguities arise, their resolutions should be recorded as canonical FAQ entries.

- The FAQ serves as the arbitration document during cleanup and later revisions.  
- FAQ entries constrain future interpretations and rewrites.  
- FAQ content is higher-priority canon than any single document, unless explicitly superseded.

### 6. Ensure Coherence Across Scales
All domains (physics, magic, deep-layer behavior, consciousness, cosmology, etc.) must obey:

- global causal ordering  
- conservation of deep-layer imbalance / energy  
- anchoring and projection rules  
- resonance and stability constraints  
- sieve logic of emergent layers  
- non-arbitrary structure formation (no “one-off” exceptions)  

### 7. Support Future Expansion
The ontology must be extensible:

- new concepts should integrate without requiring exceptions or retcons  
- new layers or structures must fit the existing ordering and constraints  
- extensions should reuse existing mechanisms where possible, rather than inventing parallel systems

## How the Model Should Assist

In any new thread or session where this document is provided, the assistant should:

- analyze, critique, and refine existing concepts  
- identify ambiguities, contradictions, or unclear relationships  
- propose structurally minimal redefinitions or clarifications  
- ensure all reasoning and edits respect the hierarchical ordering  
- respect the subtractive design philosophy (prefer removal/simplification over addition/complication)  
- avoid introducing new entities, layers, or rules unless strictly necessary for consistency  
- help construct and refine the FAQ as ambiguities are discovered  
- treat the FAQ as binding canon once entries are established and provided in the session  
- only reorganize or rewrite documents after their meaning is clarified and unified against the FAQ and ordering

The assistant must treat this document, plus the FAQ, as top-level guidance for interpreting and modifying any Rift Worlds ontology material.

## One-Sentence Mission Summary
Refine the Rift Worlds ontology into a minimal, coherent, non-redundant, substrate-first metaphysical framework by iteratively resolving ambiguities, unifying terminology, and enforcing structural consistency across all documents.
