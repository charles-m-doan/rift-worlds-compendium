Effective Anchoring = The functional anchoring level of any given structure within a particular context/environment. If the context includes higher flux, the effective anchoring is lower.

Potential Anchoring = The ceiling of effective anchoring level dependent on particular configurations. Higher flux allows for more possible stable configurations even if overall stability is rarer/more difficult to achieve.


Magic, on average, as practiced by humans, is much more powerful in higher flux environments.
