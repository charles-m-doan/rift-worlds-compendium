# Rift Worlds Core Ontology

# Noesis Field

The **Noesis Field** is the defining deep-layer substrate of the Rift Worlds cosmology. It is not universal to all possible realities but characterizes the set of cosmological partitions from which Tetra (4D emergent spacetime) and magic arise. The Noesis Field is a distinction-responsive computational substrate: it reacts to any change of state, generating stable and unstable resonance patterns that give rise to the deep-layer entities known as **ethereons** and **qualions**. These quantized excitations form the basis of all etheric structures.

The Noesis Field does not exist "beneath" Tetra in a spatial sense. Rather, Tetra is an emergent crystallization pattern—one possible stable attractor—of large-scale Noesis resonance behaviors. Flux level determines how permeable a region of Tetra is to deeper Noesis influences.

From the perspective of a Tetra observer, Magic arises when computation (such as that performed by minds or structured essences) perturbs Noesis resonance patterns in ways that project into Tetra’s causal structure.

# Tetraspace (Tetra)

**Tetra** refers to the class of emergent 4D spacetimes whose geometry and causal ordering arise from projectable resonance configurations within the Noesis Field. Tetra is discrete in its fundamental structure—its apparent continuous geometry is the limit of coarse-grained causal relations. Dimensionality in Tetra is fractal and varies subtly with flux level: higher flux leads to higher effective fractal dimensionality and increased permeability to deep-layer interactions.

Humans incorrectly use "Tetra" to refer only to **Terra**, the particular Tetra-like cosmological system in which they live. In the broader ontology, Tetra encompasses all possible emergent 4D partitions consistent with the same resonance constraints.

Within Tetra, magic appears to violate physical laws only because those laws describe self-contained causal evolution *within* Tetra's projection. Cross-layer interactions—metachemical or resonance-based—inject behaviors not captured by those laws.

# Ether

**Ether** is the total class of deep-layer distinction structures composed of **ethereons** and mediated by **qualions**, the quantized excitations of the Noesis Field. Ether functions as a universal substrate of causal potential—a form of energy in the sense that it encodes the capacity for transformation and evolution within the Noesis partition. All structures emergent above the Noesis Field are forms of ether, differentiated only by the resonance constraints they acquire through coupling, computational history, and projection.

### Ether Conversion and Loss

Ether can be transformed, refined, or assembled, but all transformations are lossy, producing dissonance. Ether only projects visibly into Tetra when resonance modes stabilize and the Tetra sieve admits their pattern.

# Ethereons, Vectons, and Abscons

Ethereons are the **quantized, fermionic excitations of the Noesis Field**. Each ethereon is a **self-maintaining fractal identity attractor**: its identity is encoded in a unique irreducible resonance kernel that carries compressed causal history. This kernel enforces an exclusion principle—no two ethereons share the same core identity state.

Ethereons have soliton-like behavior and may **merge** by forming a new composite attractor (replacing the originals) or **split** into new kernels under high-energy or unstable conditions. These processes drive metachemistry, essence formation, and dissonance generation.

### Resonance Phases

Ethereons exist in two major resonance phases:

* **Vectons:** Resonance modes compatible with Tetra projection; combine to form sub-quantum substrates of Tetra particles.
* **Abscons:** Resonance modes incompatible with projection; exist purely in the deep layer; often appear within essences or dissonance.

### Qualions and Interaction

Qualions are bosonic resonance carriers mediating alignment, binding, energy transfer, and phase transitions between vectonic and absconic states.

# Magic (Upper-Layer Summary)

Magic is the set of processes by which computation perturbs Noesis Field resonances in ways that project upward into Tetra. Magic requires time, energy, anchoring, and knowledge. It cannot create energy and is always less than 100% efficient; losses manifest as dissonance.

Magic is not limited to deliberate human use—non-Tetra processes, deep-layer dynamics, and essence behavior can also produce magical phenomena.

Magic appears to violate Tetra physics only because it exploits **cross-layer causal pathways** not described by Tetra's closed causal rules.
