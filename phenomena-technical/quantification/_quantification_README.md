# quantification

This directory contains formalized, structural, or quantitative descriptions of emergent phenomena in the Rift Worlds ontology.  
Documents here specify rules, constraints, measurable behaviors, symbolic models, or structural mappings of higher-layer systems.

Typical contents:
- stability and resonance constraints for magic or essences  
- approximate or symbolic formulations of hyperchemistry  
- Rift behavior models, thresholds, and equations  
- Tetra coupling rules  
- emergent invariants, diagrams, tables, and schemas  
- mappings between emergent phenomena and deep-layer mechanisms  

Quantification documents refine qualitative descriptions into formal, consistent system specifications.
