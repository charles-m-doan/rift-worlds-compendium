# phenomena-technical

This directory contains documents describing emergent, observable, or applied-layer phenomena within the Rift Worlds ontology.  
All content here must remain consistent with the substrate constraints defined in `meta-technical`.

This directory branches into:

- `qualitative/` — conceptual descriptions of emergent behaviors and higher-layer systems.
- `quantification/` — formalizations, rules, constraints, diagrams, and models for emergent systems.

Phenomena documented here may include:
- magic behavior as experienced in-world  
- essence effects and interactions  
- observable hyperchemical or metachemical processes  
- Rift manifestations and Tetra behavior  
- emergent field effects, patterns, or system dynamics  

These documents describe *what happens* at the emergent level, rather than the substrate machinery behind it.
