High flux is both a blessing and a curse.

In higher flux environments, the fabric of reality itself is more free. Humans and other Tetra-bound organisms have a baseline stability. Tetra is like a rock in a raging river. It is a foundation. Compared to the stability of Tetra, flux variation is a surface phenomenon, even though it can allow for deep pathways into the layers from which Tetra structures emerge. And yet, the higher the flux, the more blurred the boundary between Tetra and alternate cosomological systems--the nearest being the various layers of Eidos. Flux is first perceived as a variability in the "phase" of space.

Magic as humans understand it is the willful manipulation of identity through space and time. In higher flux, identity requires more information to specify. Thus, by default, the same 'spell' in low flux will be more strongly anchored than in high flux. The fewer degrees of freedom in low flux means that Tetra itself can contribute a larger portion of the information that specifies a "here" and "now."  Whereas in high flux, identity wants to diffuse in more directions. The higher the flux, the smaller the portion of Tetra's influence on the identity specification.

However, the greater degrees of freedom associated with higher flux mean that more configurations are possible. There are more ways to embed information from Tetra that pinpoint an identity in space and time--exponentially so. Given the right configurations, anchoring can be vastly higher, but more mana is required to specify, and unique information from Tetra becomes exponentially more important.  Additional mana spent to configure the spell is only useful for anchoring insofar as it can be entangled with patterns of causal information from Tetra--or whatever spacetime environment in which one is seeking to crystalize an identity.

Mana vs Ether (MAJOR REVISION)

Mana = crystalized energy composed of ethereons
Ether = the quantizable dimensions of the Noesis Field - it's the medium in which mana manifests

Mana derives from the ether. Basically it's my new term for "magical energy."  Not really "new" as it's pretty standard in most settings.  Anyway, everything is composed of mana--including familiar matter.  "Energy" is 'more fundamental' than mana in this perspective. They aren't really different things... but mana can be thought of as emergent patterns of energy that are locked into some meta-stable state.
