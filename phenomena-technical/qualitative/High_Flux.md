High flux is both a blessing and a curse.

In higher flux environments, the fabric of reality itself is more free. Humans and other Tetra-bound organisms have a baseline stability. Tetra is like a rock in a raging river. It is a foundation. Compared to the stability of Tetra, flux variation is a surface phenomenon, even though it can allow for deep pathways into the layers from which Tetra structures emerge. And yet, the higher the flux, the more blurred the boundary between Tetra and alternate cosomological systems--the nearest being the various layers of Eidos. Flux is first perceived as a variability in the "phase" of space.

Magic as humans understand it is the willful manipulation of identity through space and time. In higher flux, identity requires more information to specify. Thus, by default, the same 'spell' in low flux will be more strongly anchored than in high flux. The fewer degrees of freedom in low flux means that Tetra itself can contribute a larger portion of the information that specifies a "here" and "now."  Whereas in high flux, identity wants to diffuse in more directions. The higher the flux, the smaller the portion of Tetra's influence on the identity specification.

However, the greater degrees of freedom associated with higher flux mean that more configurations are possible. There are more ways to embed information from Tetra that pinpoint an identity in space and time--exponentially so. Given the right configurations, anchoring can be vastly higher, but more mana is required to specify, and unique information from Tetra becomes exponentially more important.  Additional mana spent to configure the spell is only useful for anchoring insofar as it can be entangled with patterns of causal information from Tetra--or whatever spacetime environment in which one is seeking to crystalize an identity. There are methods for maximizing the value of the causal entanglement--like extracting as much "signal" as possible from a finite data sample--and those methods depend on the extended configuration. However there are also ways to use mana to "ping" spacetime to get more data/feedback into the spell pattern.  This also has higher potential with higher flux as there are more unique potential causal pathways to get the same information--i.e. the extended dimensionality itself can begin to play a role in locating a "here" and "now" in identity space. That is, magic that is tailored to higher flux levels generally costs more mana, and that mana goes toward bootstrap scaffolding that amplifies the anchoring via extended causal structure.

The increased mana costs are significantly offset by the faster diffusion rate of dissonance, requiring less mana to be spend transmuting or siphoning away dissonance as it accumulates.
